SUPPORT

Questions, bugs or feedback: slugics@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

How do AI credits work?

The Coach, plans and recaps use AI credits. Buy credit packs or subscribe to Pulse Pro in Crew → Get more credits, no account needed. The cost of each feature is listed there. Sign in with Apple to get 50 free credits, back up your progress and use your credits on all your devices.

How do I restore purchases?

Crew → Get more credits → Restore purchases, on a device signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel Pulse Pro?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pulse Pro → Cancel. You keep your credits until the end of the period.

How do I delete my account?

Crew → Account → Delete account. This deletes your account, credits and cloud backup.

How do I change the language?

Pulse follows your iPhone's language. To change it just for Pulse: Settings → Apps → Pulse → Language.

Is my data private?

Your logs stay on your phone unless you turn on cloud backup. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.
