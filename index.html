Common questions

How do AI credits work? The Coach, plans and recaps use AI credits. New accounts get 50 free credits. You can buy credit packs or subscribe to Pulse Pro in Crew → Credits. The cost of each feature is listed on that screen.

How do I restore purchases? Crew → Credits → Restore purchases. Sign in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel Pulse Pro? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pulse Pro → Cancel. You keep your credits until the end of the period.

How do I delete my account? Crew → Account → Delete account. This deletes your account, credits and cloud backup.

How do I add friends or join a crew? Open the Squad tab, then tap Invite to send your link, or Enter code to use a friend's or crew's 8-character code. Tap any player's name to nudge, challenge, report or block them.

How do I change the language? Pulse follows your iPhone's language. To change it just for Pulse: Settings → Apps → Pulse → Language.

Pulse gives general fitness and nutrition guidance generated by AI. It isn't medical advice.
