WHAT'S IN THE BOX

Everything you need to eat right, train and stay sane, wrapped in neon and spray paint.

THE COACH

Talk, type or send a photo. Your AI coach logs meals and workouts, answers training and nutrition questions, and talks back out loud. Reach it hands-free with Siri, the Action Button or Back Tap.

📸

SNAP & SCAN

Photograph a plate for instant calories and macros, or scan a barcode. Water, weight, sleep and mood are one tap away, and Apple Health keeps your burned calories in sync.

🍽️

PLANS EVERY DAY

Tell Pulse your goal and get calorie and macro targets for your body. Fresh meal and workout plans show up every morning, built around what you actually logged.

🧠

MIND & RECOVERY

Tell the Coach how you slept and how your day went. Pulse turns it into a daily stress score and an illustrated journal, and shows how stress and sleep affect your progress.

📈

STATS & RECAPS

7-day, 30-day and all-time trends for calories, protein, weight and workouts, with tips on what to improve. Every week ends with a comic-book recap you can share.

📷

PROGRESS PICS

Add a few photos per day. Compare mode lines up your latest shot with an older one from the same angle, and Retro mode redraws your progress as 80s cartoon art.

🔥

RANKS & 30 WALLS

Every log earns XP. Climb 15 Street Crew ranks from Rookie to Pulse Immortal and unlock 30 illustrated walls that become the background of the whole app.

🎯

SUPPORT

Questions, bugs or feedback: slugics@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

How do AI credits work?

The Coach, plans and recaps use AI credits. Buy credit packs or subscribe to Pulse Pro in Crew → Get more credits, no account needed. The cost of each feature is listed there. Sign in with Apple to get 50 free credits, back up your progress and use your credits on all your devices.

How do I restore purchases?

Crew → Get more credits → Restore purchases, on a device signed in to the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel Pulse Pro?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pulse Pro → Cancel. You keep your credits until the end of the period.

How do I delete my account?

Crew → Account → Delete account. This deletes your account, credits and cloud backup.

How do I change the language?

Pulse follows your iPhone's language. To change it just for Pulse: Settings → Apps → Pulse → Language.

Is my data private?

Your logs stay on your phone unless you turn on cloud backup. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.
