SUPPORT

How do AI credits work?

The Coach, plans and recaps use AI credits. New accounts get 50 free credits. You can buy credit packs or subscribe to Pulse Pro in Crew → Credits, where the cost of each feature is listed. You can also earn free credits by inviting friends or trading XP.

How do I restore purchases?

Crew → Credits → Restore purchases. Sign in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel Pulse Pro?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pulse Pro → Cancel. You keep your credits until the end of the period.

How do I delete my account?

Crew → Account → Delete account. This deletes your account, credits and cloud backup.

How do I change the language?

Pulse follows your iPhone's language. To change it just for Pulse: Settings → Apps → Pulse → Language.

Is my data private?

Your logs stay on your phone unless you turn on cloud backup. Read the Privacy Policy for the details.

Pulse gives general fitness and nutrition guidance generated by AI. It isn't medical advice.
